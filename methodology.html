Methodology

How we identify, verify, and classify Nigeria's philanthropic organisations.

Data Collection

Data partner: NNNGO

The registry data was collected and compiled by NNNGO through a systematic survey of the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) of Nigeria's public database. The CAC is Nigeria's official regulatory body for the incorporation of companies and registration of non-profit organisations, making it the authoritative and verifiable source for philanthropic entities operating across the country.

Researchers cross-referenced CAC registration records to identify organisations engaged in philanthropic, civil society, or corporate social responsibility activities, covering all six geopolitical zones and 36 states.

Inclusion Criteria

Organisations were included in the registry if they met one or more of the following criteria:

  • Registered with the CAC as a foundation, trust, or non-governmental organisation
  • Primary mandate focused on philanthropic giving, social investment, or community development
  • Operating in one or more of the recognised impact sectors (health, education, agriculture, governance, climate, etc.)
  • Corporate foundations or CSR bodies with a dedicated, structured giving programme
  • Faith-based organisations with a demonstrable community development or social welfare function

Classification Framework

Each organisation in the registry is classified across four dimensions:

  • Foundation Classification — Private Foundation, Corporate Foundation, Faith-Based Foundation, Community Trust, or Donor-Advised Fund
  • Geography — Geopolitical zone and state of primary operation
  • CSR / Philanthropy Focus Areas — the sectors and thematic areas the organisation works in
  • GESI Tags — Gender, Equity, and Social Inclusion markers (Women-Led, Disability-Focused, Minority-Serving) where applicable

Data Quality & Verification

Each record was reviewed against publicly available sources, including the CAC portal, organisation websites, and published annual reports, to verify registration status, governance structure, and areas of focus. CAC Registration Status is recorded as Active or Inactive based on the organisation's standing at the time of data collection.

All data is handled in accordance with Nigeria's Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). Personal data is limited to publicly available organisational information and no individual-level data is stored.

Ongoing Updates

The registry is maintained by Gatefield on behalf of the TY Danjuma Foundation. Organisations can submit updates or corrections using the Submit Organisation form. All submissions are reviewed before publication. Gatefield conducts periodic re-verification of existing records to ensure registration status and organisational details remain current.
