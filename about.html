About

Leading coordination, transparency, and collaboration across Nigeria's philanthropic ecosystem.

The Nigeria Philanthropy Registry is a centralised, searchable registry of foundations, NGOs, and corporate social responsibility (CSR) bodies operating across Nigeria's six geopolitical zones.

Commissioned by the TY Danjuma Foundation, this platform is designed to serve as critical infrastructure for the social sector. By mapping the ecosystem of funders, it enables policymakers, civil society organisations, and peer foundations to discover synergies, avoid duplication of efforts, and foster strategic, high-impact partnerships.

Our methodology ensures that data is Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) compliant, accurate, and consistently updated. From health and education to climate action and governance, the registry captures over 15 impact sectors, tracking GESI (Gender and Social Inclusion) indicators to highlight marginalised and vulnerable communities.

This registry is open source and belongs to the Nigeria Philanthropy Ecosystem. It was commissioned by the TY Danjuma Foundation and is currently administered by Gatefield.
